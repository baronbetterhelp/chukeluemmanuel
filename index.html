About

One identity.

Chukelu Chekwube Emmanuel, professionally known as World Famous Baron, is a Nigerian entrepreneur, businessman, procurement professional and web developer.

He is the founder and Chief Executive Officer of Uni Lodge Finder, a digital platform focused on helping university students discover accommodation, and the Chief Executive Officer of Baron Corporation.

He also leads Baron Lifestyle, a digital media, news and lifestyle brand. His work spans procurement, technology, entrepreneurship, web development and digital business development.
